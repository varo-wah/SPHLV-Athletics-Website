# ACSC logo provenance — October 21, 2026

All nine images were downloaded from the images displayed by the [official ACSC conference homepage](https://acscconference.com/) on October 21, 2026. Its member-school directory explicitly associates each school with the corresponding image. The footer supplies the conference logo. No generated, traced, recolored, or unofficial artwork is used.

The PNG files are the site's own 300 × 300 WordPress CDN renditions (`https://i0.wp.com/acscconference.com/wp-content/uploads/...?...`). They were saved without alteration. All nine PNG signatures and dimensions were checked and every downloaded image was visually inspected. The set is approximately 127 KB total. Existing transparency and white backgrounds are preserved.

The authentic SPH image from the conference directory is used within ACSC contexts; the existing LV logo elsewhere is unchanged.

The artwork remains attributable to ACSC and its member schools. Downloading assets for this user-authorized athletics website does not establish an independent license to redistribute their branding for unrelated uses.
